TF.NX @VERSION@ is built from https://github.com/belacmu/tf-nx, commit @COMMIT@. That repository is its full source
(translator and generated code included), under GPL-3.0.

Components:
- TF.EX (Fcornaire and contributors), GPL-3.0: translated into the module; its files in xplay/tfex/.
- TF.State (TF.EX's), GPL-3.0: translated.
- FortRise (Terria / FortRise team), MIT: its content loader and registries translated.
- exlaunch (shadowninja108), GPL-2.0: the module's loader layer. It states no GPL version for itself (only the
  licence text), which GPL-2.0 section 9 reads as any version, so it is used here under GPL-3.0 with the rest.
- And64InlineHook (Rprop), MIT; libnx (switchbrew), ISC; oss-rtld (Thog), ISC.
- GGRS (Fcornaire's fork), matchbox and the Rust crates in ggrs-switch and tfx-rtc: MIT / Apache-2.0.
- Teams+ (ours), in xplay/mods/TeamsPlus.
